❤️ About Love Care Global Home Nursing

Connecting Ghana to Verified Nurses - Anytime, Anywhere

Love Care Global Home Nursing is Ghana's first on-demand home nursing platform. Founded 2025 in Accra, we solve the gap between families needing professional care and qualified nurses seeking flexible work.

What We Do

  • 🏥 Home Nursing - Elderly care, post-surgery, injections, wound care
  • 🚨 SOS Emergency - One-tap emergency alert with location to nearby nurses & contacts
  • 👩‍⚕️ Verified Nurses Only - ID, certificate, video verification + CEO approval
  • 💬 Real-time Booking & Group Chat for care coordination

Mission

Affordable, trusted, verified nursing care for every Ghanaian family.
