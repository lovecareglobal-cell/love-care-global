About Love Care Global Home Nursing

Connecting 8 Countries to Verified Nurses - Anytime, Anywhere

Love Care Global Home Nursing is Africa's first on-demand home nursing platform, now scaling to 8 countries. Founded 2025 in Accra, Ghana, we solve the critical gap between families needing trusted professional care and qualified nurses seeking flexible, dignified work.

What We Do

  • 🏥 Home Nursing - Elderly care, post-surgery, injections, wound care
  • 🚨 SOS Emergency - One-tap emergency alert with location to nearby nurses & contacts
  • 👩‍⚕️ Verified Nurses Only - ID, certificate, video verification + CEO approval
  • 💬 Real-time Booking & Group Chat for care coordination

Mission

Affordable, trusted, verified nursing care for every family in our 8 countries: Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, USA, UK, Canada, Australia.
