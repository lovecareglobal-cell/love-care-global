LOVE CARE GLOBAL ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our app and website at https://lovecareglobal-cell.github.io/love-care-global.

1. Data We Collect

  • Phone number, name, profile photo
  • Location (only when you allow - for finding nearby nurses and SOS emergency)
  • Booking details, service type, date/time, address
  • Ratings, reviews, SOS alerts
  • Nurse verification: ID, certificates, video intro (for nurses)

2. How We Use Data

To connect clients with verified nurses, process bookings via Paystack, handle SOS emergencies, provide group chat for staff coordination, improve service.

3. Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. Data is shared only with: your booked nurse for service delivery, Paystack for payments, Firebase (Google) for secure storage. SOS data may be shared with emergency contacts you provide.

4. Firebase & Security

We use Firebase Realtime Database with production security rules, encrypted in transit. Access restricted to authenticated users.

5. Your Rights (GDPR Compliant)

You can request data export or deletion anytime via:

  • In-app: Settings > Delete Account
  • Online: https://lovecareglobal-cell.github.io/love-care-global/delete-account.html
  • Email: lovecareglobal@gmail.com

6. Location Permission

Location is optional. Used only for "Find Nearby Nurses" and SOS. You can deny permission in phone settings. App works without it.

7. Children

App not for children under 18. Nurses must be 18+ with verified credentials.
