📜 NURSE POLICY - Unique for Nurses - Read & Scroll

1. Engagement & Qualification

You are engaged as independent licensed nurse/caregiver through LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING. You confirm you have valid license/PIN/certificate in good standing, no criminal record, physically fit. You will only perform duties within your scope and doctor's orders.

2. Zero Tolerance Code of Conduct - Dismissal + Legal Action if Breach

A) NO EXTRA MONEY: You will NEVER collect cash, MoMo, bank transfer, gifts, tips, drugs money from client/family. ALL payments must go through official LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING company account. If client offers money, you must refuse and report. If caught collecting extra money, instant dismissal, report to Nursing Council, police, and you repay double.

B) NO SEXUAL/ROMANTIC RELATIONSHIP: Zero tolerance - No sexual, romantic, intimate relationship with client, family, or household. No sexual harassment, touching, comments. Instant dismissal + report.

C) NO THEFT/FRAUD: No stealing client money, jewelry, food, drugs, medical supplies, or falsifying vital signs, records. No using client phone, money, property.

D) NO SLEEPING ON DUTY / NO PHONE ABUSE: You must stay alert. No sleeping during shift, no TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp chatting, calls, movies while on duty. Phone only for work or emergency. If night shift, stay awake and monitor patient.

E) Professionalism: Wear full uniform + ID, be punctual (arrive 15mins before), greet respectfully, maintain hygiene, respect privacy, dignity, culture, religion. No smoking, alcohol, drugs on duty.

3. Clinical Duties

Give only prescribed medication per doctor order - check name, dose, expiry. Monitor and record vital signs accurately. Provide wound care, hygiene, feeding, mobilization as per care plan. Report any change, fall, emergency, complaint immediately to supervisor via call + written report within 1 hour.

4. Financial Accountability - Receipts Mandatory

If client gives you money to buy drugs, food, transport, you MUST: Take permission from supervisor, Collect receipt, Send receipt + balance + photo of drugs same day via WhatsApp to supervisor. Any shortage, loss, no receipt = you repay. For accountability because you handle clients and money, company will collect your Next of Kin and Guarantor details. They will be contacted if you cause financial loss or misconduct under laws of your country.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Compete 12 Months - Very Important

Keep ALL client information confidential forever - name, address, health, family. Do NOT post client photos, videos, info on TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp status. For 12 MONTHS after you stop working with LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING, you will NOT contact, solicit, or work privately for any client you served through us, whether they call you or you call them. If you breach, you agree to pay 6 months service fees as damages. This protects clients and our business.

6. Data Protection

Your personal data, license, ID, phone, Next of Kin, Guarantor will be kept confidential under Data Protection Laws, used only for assignment, payroll, compliance.

7. Termination

We may terminate immediately without notice for: extra money collection, sexual misconduct, theft, sleeping on duty, falsifying records, client abuse, no-show. Otherwise 2 weeks notice.
