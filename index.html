• Hourly: total hours at home

• Weekly: weeks x 7 days
• Monthly: months x 30 days

💰 What is the legal minimum pay? Ghana 6.04 GHS/hr (65% OFF +15%) +

Ghana Law PROMO: 6.04 GHS per hour (15 GHS -65% = 5.25 +15% = 6.04). Formula: Total Hours × 5.25 GHS = Legal Minimum Total. Example: 7 days x 8 hrs = 56 hrs → 56×5.25 = 294 GHS minimum (was 840). All 32 countries reduced 65%.
