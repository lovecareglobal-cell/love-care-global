🏠 Care Mode - Live-in vs Live-out

⏰ Live-out: Set Start & End Time (Daily)

For Live-out, nurse comes at Start Time and leaves at End Time daily. Hours/day auto-calculated.

Hours/day: 6 hrs (08:00 - 14:00)
Nurse will work these exact hours daily. Night shift allowed (e.g. 20:00 - 06:00 = 10 hrs)
Live-out: Nurse works fixed hours per day and returns home. Pay per hour. Example 2hrs/day × 7 days = 14 hrs total.
Live-in: Nurse stays 24hrs, 8hrs active + on-call night. Requires: Private room, Food, Toilet/Bath, Rest time. Charged per day + 10% allowance.
