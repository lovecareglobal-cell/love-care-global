1. Introduction

LOVE CARE GLOBAL ("we", "us") provides verified nurse booking services in Ghana, Nigeria, and Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Contact: Phone number, name, email
  • Location: GPS for nearby nurses and SOS emergency (with permission)
  • Bookings: Date, service type, price, nurse assigned
  • Verification (Nurses): ID, certificates, video, phone
  • Chat: Group chat messages (nurses/staff/CEO only)
  • SOS: lat, lng, time, phone when SOS pressed

3. How We Use Data

To match clients with nearest verified nurses, process bookings, enable SOS alerts, provide group chat for internal staff, and improve service. We do NOT sell data.

4. Data Storage - Firebase

Data is stored securely in Google Firebase Realtime Database (US/EU) with production security rules: root read/write false, per-node validation, 1000 char limit, timestamp validation.

5. Data Sharing

Only shared with: booked nurse, assigned staff, CEO for service delivery. Not shared with advertisers.

6. Your Rights

You have right to: access, export, correct, delete your data. Use in-app Delete Account page (Settings > Delete Account) or email lovecareglobal@gmail.com. Deletion within 48h.

7. Location Permission

Location is used only for: finding nearby nurses, SOS emergency alert, live nurse tracking. You can deny permission and manually enter address. Location not tracked in background when app closed.

8. Children

App not intended for under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Security

Firebase rules + HTTPS + rate-limit 3s + anti-flood 10/min + XSS sanitization + 1000 char max. Group chat restricted to verified roles.

10. Retention

Bookings kept 2 years for legal. Chat messages 1 year. SOS alerts 1 year. Deleted account data removed within 48h, backups within 90 days.
