Privacy Policy - LOVE CARE GLOBAL

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

LOVE CARE GLOBAL connects clients in Ghana to licensed home nurses.

2. Data We Collect

Name, phone number, email, GPS location, care type requested, booking history.

3. Why We Collect

To find closest nurse, track arrival, process payment via Paystack, safety.

4. Location Data

We collect precise location only when you book or when nurse is on duty. Background location is used only for active duty nurses to show live tracking to client.

5. Sharing

We share your name, phone and location with assigned nurse only. We do not sell data. Paystack handles payments securely.

6. Security

Data encrypted in transit and at rest via Firebase.

7. Your Rights / Delete Account

You can delete your account anytime: In app go to Settings > Delete Account, or email us at lovecareglobal@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Account". We will delete within 48 hours.

8. Contact

lovecareglobal@gmail.com - Accra, Ghana

9. Medical Disclaimer

We are a platform, not a hospital. For emergency, call 112.
