1. Who We Are

LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING is a licensed home nursing agency operating in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Uganda, Rwanda, UK, USA and other countries. We provide qualified nurses and caregivers to care for you at home.

2. What Service You Will Receive

Based on your selection, we will assign a qualified licensed nurse for: General nursing, Elderly care, Post-surgery wound dressing, Mother & baby care, Injections/IV/Vital signs, 24-hour live-in, Day or Night shift. Nurse will follow doctor's orders and care plan.

3. Your Responsibilities as Client

Provide true health history, allergies, medications, doctor contact. Provide safe, clean environment, access to water, electricity, toilet for nurse. Provide necessary equipment (bed, gloves, etc.). Respect nurse - No verbal abuse, sexual harassment, or discrimination. Allow nurse to take short breaks.

4. Payment Policy - Very Important

All fees must be paid IN ADVANCE to official LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING company account only (Bank transfer / MoMo / Official link). NEVER pay cash to nurse. We issue receipt. If you pay nurse directly, we are not responsible and you will pay again. 24-hour cancellation notice required. If you cancel less than 24hrs or nurse arrives and you cancel, 50% charge applies. No refund after service starts unless nurse did not show or misconduct proven.

5. Emergency Policy

Home nursing is NOT ambulance or hospital emergency. Our nurses provide basic first aid, monitor, call ambulance, contact your family and doctor. You are responsible for ambulance and hospital bills. Nurse may refuse to perform procedures beyond nursing scope or unsafe without doctor order.

6. Privacy & Data Protection - Your Data Safe

Your name, phone, email, address, health info, GPS will be kept strictly confidential under Data Protection Laws of your country and GDPR where applicable. Used only for care delivery, nurse assignment, and legal. We never sell your data. You can request to see or correct your data.

7. Liability Limit

We provide qualified staff but do NOT guarantee cure, recovery, or outcome. Our liability is limited to service fees you paid for that period. We are not liable for pre-existing conditions, complications, client refusing advice, or force majeure.

8. Complaints & Replacement

If nurse is late, unprofessional, or you are not satisfied, report within 24hrs via official WhatsApp/Email. We will investigate and replace nurse if needed at no extra charge for that shift.
