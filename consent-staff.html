All phones, laptops, ID cards, uniforms, fuel cards, keys, documents are company property. Return ALL on resignation/termination day. You are liable for loss/damage.

5. Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation 12 Months

For 12 MONTHS after leaving LOVE CARE GLOBAL HOME NURSING, you will NOT: Start or work for competing home nursing agency using our clients/nurses/data, Solicit our clients to leave us, Solicit our nurses/staff to leave us. If you breach, you agree to pay 6 months of your last salary as damages.

7. Work Ethics

Be punctual, honest, respectful to clients, nurses, colleagues. Report any theft, fraud, misconduct you see. No alcohol/drugs at work. Follow manager instructions.

Your personal data, ID, contact, Next of Kin, Guarantor will be kept confidential under Data Protection Laws, used only for HR, payroll, legal compliance.
